How the intelligent generator works

The system analyzes course offerings, lecturers, levels, venues, semesters and available time slots. It then assigns courses while checking timetable constraints and preventing scheduling conflicts.

Scheduling Features
Conflict Detection Prevent lecturer, venue and level clashes.
Time Allocation Assign courses to suitable available time slots.
Venue Assignment Select appropriate venues for scheduled classes.
Lecturer Scheduling Prevent lecturers from being scheduled simultaneously.
Level Protection Prevent a level from having overlapping classes.
Intelligent Selection Prioritize difficult scheduling combinations first.
Intelligent Scheduling Approach

The timetable generator uses a constraint-based heuristic approach. Hard constraints are checked first to eliminate invalid assignments, while scheduling preferences are considered when selecting among valid alternatives.

Scheduling Constraints

Rules enforced by the generator

  • A lecturer cannot teach two classes at the same time.
  • A venue cannot host two classes at the same time.
  • A level cannot have overlapping classes.
  • Every course offering must have a valid lecturer and level.
  • Courses must be assigned to available time slots.
  • Venue assignments must satisfy timetable requirements.
